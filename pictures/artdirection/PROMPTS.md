# SUPERCUT art direction

Eight original concepts made with the built-in image generation tool. One try per picture, no edits or repaints. Each saved PNG was opened and visually inspected. Native output is 1672 x 941, approximately 16:9; no resizing was applied.

Layout is consistent in broad placement, but independent generations shift camera, scale, costumes and prop details. These are direction comparisons, not pixel-matched live/frozen pairs.

## Exact prompts

For every picture the exact submitted prompt is the shared block below, followed by one newline, followed by that picture's block. Blocks exclude the Markdown fences. No input images were supplied.

Shared block:

```text
Create one original finished-game screenshot concept, landscape 16:9, target 1920x1080. First-person eye-level wide camera looking straight down a sound-stage set, horizon at 48 percent height. Fixed composition for art comparison: left gloved hand and blank striped clapperboard occupy bottom-left corner (x 5-30%, y 72-98%); right gloved hand gripping a clearly recognizable six-shot revolver occupies bottom-right (x 71-96%, y 72-98%), barrel aims toward center. BOTH HANDS CLEARLY VISIBLE. Central aisle leads to a rectangular green-lit exit gate centered at x 52%, y 41%. Exactly three enemies: ordinary shooter at x 43%, y 53% mid distance, firing one slow bright red-orange projectile toward the camera at x 46%, y 61%; heavy charging enemy at x 24%, y 56% leaning into a stride; thin distant sniper on elevated scaffold at x 76%, y 33%, with a thin red-orange aim line directed toward the foreground. Large wind machine, propeller clearly inside circular wire guard, at x 83%, y 58%. Film-set walls on both sides with rear braces exposed, scaffold right, lamps on stands, dark soundstage roof above. No interface, crosshair, border, text, lettering, numbers, labels, logos, trademarks, barcode, QR, real-person likeness, recognizable borrowed IP. Clapperboard is completely blank except diagonal stripes. Enemies have NO human faces or skin. Original art only. Maintain all specified positions.
```
